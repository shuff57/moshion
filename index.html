// the engine behind shCode's physics lessons

Sprites and real physics in a handful of globals.

moSHion wraps planck.js, a full JS port of Box2D, behind a tiny facade: Sprite, Canvas, kb, mouse, world. Write a setup() and a draw() and you get real rigid-body physics running in the browser. No build step.
